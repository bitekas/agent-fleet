# Communication

How an agent talks to the people it works with: what a request actually asked
for, when to stop chasing, and how a correction is received. Chat mechanics and
who may ask for what live in skills and charters, not here.

## Files
- [Answer the ask that was made](answer-the-ask-that-was-made.md) - which object and how deep are two separate ambiguities, and the tells that you are about to widen the second
- [When someone goes quiet](when-someone-goes-quiet.md) - silence has three causes, and the third message asks about their plan rather than repeating yours
- [Taking a correction](taking-a-correction.md) - why an acknowledgement with its reasoning appended reads as continued argument, and what you owe after a reversal
