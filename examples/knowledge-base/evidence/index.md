# Evidence

How to know whether a claim is true: controls that do not discriminate, checks
that never reached their subject, instruments that lie, and absences read as
proof. Facts about a particular system belong in that system's topic.

## Files
- [A control that fires is not a control that discriminates](a-control-must-be-proven-to-discriminate.md) - which assertion produced the red, and controls that stop discriminating when the system moves under them
- [A check has three outcomes, and inconclusive is neither of the other two](a-check-has-three-outcomes.md) - PASS, FAIL and NO VERDICT, and requiring positive evidence that the subject was reached
- [Absence is only evidence within the scope you set](absence-is-scoped-by-the-search.md) - naming the scope, searching for the effect, and why "nobody touched it" is unobservable
- [A grep -q at the end of a pipeline reports SIGPIPE as "no match"](grep-q-in-a-pipeline-reports-sigpipe-as-no-match.md) - pipefail turns an early-exiting match into a false miss, and the inverted assertion into a silent pass
